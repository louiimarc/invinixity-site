## Dramaturgical alignment

The announcement frames the body as a site where culture, technology, memory, gender, capitalism and algorithms install updates without permission. It asks who wrote the code, who edits it and what happens when the update is refused. The current visual system answers that framing with:

| Public framing | Technical/visual expression |
|---|---|
| involuntary updates and inherited code | persistent 0–99.9% installation state, pressure-controlled progress with fragment safety bands, error language and the 43-step update slideshow |
| body as biological computer | detector overlays, anatomy/pelvis imagery, mapped body and face regions |
| culture, labor and capitalism as installed systems | vocabulary sequence and 50/30/20 budgeting spreadsheet on the floor |
| projection, video and digital movement meeting the body | cue-aware video, Bebe/Kala dual mapping and p5 generative layers |
| non-human behavior | one Gamepad-controlled directional fish crossing three physical iPad aquariums |
| refusal, interruption and incompletion | progress moves only while the operator holds RT/R2 forward or LT/L2 reverse; dramaturgical timing remains human-controlled rather than automated |

The [Kala reference](https://www.instagram.com/p/DZSD9C5GNgk/) describes humans as possible biological computers running inherited instructions, and names the performance approach as postdramatic and intermedial: bodies, projection, video, digital movement and non-human behavior meet without promising a final answer. That is consistent with the architecture: the Mac coordinates state, but performers and stage management retain timing authority; generative systems expose processes rather than resolving them.
